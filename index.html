I'm Caspar. I study Information Engineering at TUM Campus Heilbronn, after a detour through business studies in Münster and public sector consulting at KPMG.

It started in 10th grade with a weather station that refused to work, until a few adults gave us their time and patience with nothing to gain from it. Since then, technology has meant one thing to me: a lever for helping people.

The place where I think that lever matters most is healthcare. That's where I want to build, eventually my own company. Until then, I build something every week and write about what I learn.
