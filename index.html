I'm Caspar. I study Information Engineering at TUM Campus Heilbronn. First I studied business in Münster, but decided I want to build something technical myself.

I think tech people are the new rockstars, and building tech is probably the second coolest thing you can do after playing James Bond. For me the fascination started in 10th grade with a weather station that refused to work, until a few adults gave us their time and patience with nothing to gain from it. Since then, technology has meant one thing to me: a lever for helping people.

The place where I think that lever matters most is healthcare. That's where I want to build, eventually my own company. Until then, I build something every week and share what I learn. Right now it's everything around robotics. I hate it when people call something "THE next big thing", but I can't argue that it might just be true for robotics.
